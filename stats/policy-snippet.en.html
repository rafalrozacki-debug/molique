Anonymous visit statistics

We keep our own anonymous traffic statistics in order to understand interest in our content and improve it. These statistics work without cookies and without storing any data in your browser. We do not identify individuals, build profiles, or track you across websites.

For each page view or download we record only aggregated, non-identifying data:

  • the address of the page visited,
  • the domain of the referring site (if you arrived from another website),
  • general technical information: device type, browser family, operating system, and approximate screen width,
  • a two-letter country code, determined locally on our server — your IP address is never stored or shared with anyone in the process,
  • the date and time of the event.

We estimate the number of unique visitors using a one-way hash that changes every day, so it cannot link the same person's visits across days or identify them. The data is not shared with any third party and stays on our server. The processing is based on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in keeping basic statistics for our own website.
