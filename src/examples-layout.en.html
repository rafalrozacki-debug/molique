Grid & Layout

Ready-to-copy molique grid patterns — from the automatic Auto-Grid, through the rigid 12-column grid and overlapping cards, to Flexbox and the asymmetric Bento Grid.

Smart Auto-Grid (RAM)

The .grid-auto class automatically arranges elements into columns — if there's no room, they drop to a new line on their own. Zero Media Queries!

Card 1
Card 2
Card 3 
<!-- Elements arrange themselves into 1, 2, or 3 columns depending on the screen -->
<div class="grid-auto gap-4">
  <div class="card">Card 1</div>
  <div class="card">Card 2</div>
  <div class="card">Card 3</div>
</div>

Rigid Grid (12-column)

When you need precise control — the .grid-cols-12 class on the parent and .col-span-* on the children.

.col-md-span-8 (66%)
.col-md-span-4 (33%) 
<!-- 1 column on mobile, 12 columns from tablet up -->
<div class="grid-cols-1 grid-md-cols-12 gap-4">

  <!-- Takes up 8 columns on desktop (100% on mobile) -->
  <div class="col-md-span-8">Main content</div>

  <!-- Takes up 4 columns on desktop (100% on mobile) -->
  <div class="col-md-span-4">Sidebar</div>

</div>

Overlapping Cards

A common Premium pattern — a card "rides up" onto the section above (e.g. a search bar over a Hero photo).

Hero Section

Overlapping card (.overlap-container) 

<!-- Section with large bottom padding (pb-5) -->
<section class="bg-dark pb-5">...</section>

<!-- Card that rides up onto the section above -->
<div class="container overlap-container">
  <div class="card shadow-lg">
    Card content
  </div>
</div>
