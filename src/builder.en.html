How it works

Dependencies are picked automatically

Some components rely on others - e.g. the confirmation modal uses the card and buttons. Selecting such a module automatically pulls in whatever it needs.

Order doesn't matter

molique declares the @layer order upfront, so precedence is decided by the declaration, not by the order you assemble the pieces in. That makes building a package from building blocks safe.

Risk-free, unlike purging

Here you decide what goes in. PurgeCSS guesses based on your files and, without a safelist, can strip out working components.
