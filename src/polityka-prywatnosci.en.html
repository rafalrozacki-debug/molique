Privacy Policy

Short and plain - what data we process on molique.dev and why.

Controller

The controller of molique.dev and the data described in this policy is Rafał Różacki (rozacki.com). For privacy-related questions, write to rafal [at] rozacki.com.

Anonymous visit statistics

We run our own, anonymous traffic statistics to understand interest in our content and improve it. The statistics work without cookies and without storing any data in your browser. We don't identify individuals, profile you, or track you across sites.

For every page view, file download, and click on a link leading away from this site, we store only aggregated, non-identifying data:

  • the address of the visited page,
  • the domain of the referring site (if you arrived from another website),
  • the name of the downloaded file, or the address (domain and path, without any parameters) of the external link that was clicked,
  • general technical information: device type, browser family, operating system, and approximate screen width,
  • a two-letter country code, determined locally on our server - your IP address is never stored or sent anywhere,
  • the date and time of the event.

We estimate unique visitors using an irreversible hash that changes every day, so it cannot link the same person's visits across different days or identify them. The data isn't shared with third parties and stays on our own server. The legal basis for processing is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in running basic statistics for our own website.

Email correspondence

If you write to us (e.g. at the address above), we process the data you provide only to reply to you. The legal basis for processing is our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in handling correspondence. We don't share this data with third parties.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict processing, and to object, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with the Polish Data Protection Authority (UODO), which has jurisdiction over the controller. Since the statistics described above are fully anonymous (they cannot be linked to a specific person), these rights apply in practice mainly to data provided in email correspondence.
