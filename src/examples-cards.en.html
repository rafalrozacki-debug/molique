Cards

Four ready-to-use molique card variants — from classic to interactive with a 3D effect — built on Flexbox with automatically aligned footers.

Classic Card

Made up of a .card container plus optional elements: .card-header, .card-body and .card-footer.

Card Title

This is a standard card. Flexbox inside takes care of laying out the content nicely.

Card with longer text

This card has more text. Notice how the footer in the card next to it automatically dropped down, so both cards end up the same height in the Grid! 

<div class="card shadow-sm">
  <div class="card-header">Card Title</div>
  <div class="card-body">
    <p>Card content...</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card-footer">
    <button class="btn btn-primary">Action</button>
  </div>
</div>

Featured Box

A special card variant designed to showcase product or service features. It has a thick top border and room for an icon.

Performance

The framework is extremely lightweight, which translates into lightning-fast page loads.

Effectiveness
