Quick Start

Unzip, copy css/ and js/ into your project, and include two files. Full example: starter.html in the package. 

<!-- in <head> -->
<link rel="stylesheet" href="css/molique-style.min.css" />

<!-- at the end of <body> (module autoloader) -->
<script defer src="js/molique-script.js"></script>
Theme: pick colors, typography, and other variables visually in the theme editor, then paste the result as a :root block. Fonts: if you checked the fonts option when downloading, just copy the fonts/ folder next to css/ - the @font-face rules in molique-style.css already point to them. Without that option, grab Inter + Poppins yourself from fonts.google.com.
