Funnels

Three conversion funnel variants - vertical, horizontal process-style (CRM), and trapezoidal with a date-range nav.

Conversion Funnel (Data Funnel)

A classic, vertical conversion funnel. Each stage's width is controlled by the --val variable. Ideal for showing lead drop-off.

Site Visits 10,000
Interest (Leads) 7,500
Qualification (MQL) 4,500
Offers Sent 2,000
Deals Won 800 
<div class="chart-funnel">
  <div class="funnel-stage" style="--val: 100%; --stage-bg: var(--primary);">
    <span>Site Visits</span>
    <strong>10,000</strong>
  </div>
  <div class="funnel-stage" style="--val: 75%; --stage-bg: var(--info); --stage-text: var(--btn-text-dark);">
    <span>Interest (Leads)</span>
    <strong>7,500</strong>
  </div>
  <!-- ... further stages ... -->
</div>

Process Funnel (CRM Pipeline)

A horizontal "chevron" layout, familiar from systems like Pipedrive or Salesforce. Automatically switches to a vertical list on mobile.

New Lead
Contact
Quote
Negotiation
Contract

True Funnel & Navigation

A trapezoid-shaped funnel variant (uses clip-path). Equipped with an elegant navigation (Segmented Control) built 100% on CSS (no JS), ideal for switching date ranges.

3,200 Leads (MQL)
