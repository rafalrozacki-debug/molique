Stock Bar (Segmented Stock Level)

A five-segment level indicator — stock levels, limits, quotas. Zero JS and zero extra markup: the segments are drawn by an SVG mask, and the number of filled segments is passed from the backend via the style="--stock-filled: 3" variable (0–5). Empty segments are a tint of the variant color, so the component also works in dark mode. Provide the value as adjacent text (or add role="img" + aria-label) — the bar itself is purely visual.

50/142 · high stock
30/96 · medium stock
30/30 · low stock
neutral variant (secondary)
